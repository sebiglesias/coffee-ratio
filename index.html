Frequently Asked Questions

Why is weighing coffee in grams better than tablespoons?

Coffee beans vary significantly in density depending on origin and roast profile. Dark roasts are lighter and less dense than light roasts; 2 tablespoons of dark roast might weigh 11 grams while light roast weighs 15 grams. A $15 digital gram scale guarantees consistent extraction every morning.

What is the coffee "Bloom" phase?

Fresh roasted coffee contains trapped carbon dioxide gas ($CO_2$). Pouring twice the weight of coffee in hot water (e.g. 50g water for 25g coffee) and waiting 45 seconds lets the gases release, ensuring the water extracts soluble coffee compounds evenly without channeling.
